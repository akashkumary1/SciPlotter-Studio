SciPlotter Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026. Applies to Android version 1.0.0, package com.sciplotter.studio.

Overview

SciPlotter Studio is an offline scientific plotting and analysis application. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs or a developer-operated backend.

Data on your device

The app processes measurement files that you choose using Android's system file picker. Imported data, plots, results and project autosave are held in app-private storage and memory. The app does not request broad storage access, contacts, location, camera, microphone or phone permissions. It has no internet permission and does not automatically transmit your measurements or usage information to the developer.

Exports and third-party document providers

You decide when and where to export a graph, workbook, report or project. If you choose a cloud document provider in the system picker, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not select or upload to a cloud service on your behalf without that user action.

Retention and deletion

Your latest project is autosaved locally. Starting a new project replaces that active autosave; session Undo is memory-limited and is not a permanent backup. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its private data. Files you deliberately exported remain in their selected location until you delete them there. Android backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app; retain your own exported project copies.

Children and scientific use

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else through the application. It is a research and educational tool, not a medical or safety-critical decision system.
